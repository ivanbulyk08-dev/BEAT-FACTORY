# Промпти для 10 кімнат (стиль Dark Rage)

Як користуватися:
- Для кожної кімнати копіюй **СПІЛЬНИЙ СТИЛЬ**, потім **ПРАВИЛА КОМПОЗИЦІЇ**, потім опис самої кімнати.
- **NEGATIVE** вставляй у поле негативного промпту, а якщо такого поля нема — додай у кінці текстом «Avoid: …».
- Промпти англійською, бо генератори картинок розуміють її краще.
- Формат: вертикальний 9:16, 1440×2560 (або 1080×1920 і потім збільшити).

## СПІЛЬНИЙ СТИЛЬ (вставляти в кожен промпт)
```
Stylized 2D mobile game background art, same style as a premium hip-hop idle game: clean bold dark outlines, cel shading with soft gradients, anime-influenced urban illustration, "dark rage" underground rap aesthetic. Palette: deep blacks and dark violet base, glowing neon purple (#9B30FF) and magenta accents, small touches of cyan and warm amber lamp light, silver/chrome details. Gothic cross and spiky star motifs used as decor. High contrast, moody night lighting, crisp readable shapes, rich small props, polished game key-art quality. Interior, empty room, nobody inside.
```

## ПРАВИЛА КОМПОЗИЦІЇ (вставляти в кожен промпт)
```
Vertical 9:16 portrait composition for a phone screen, eye-level camera slightly from above, 3/4 view into the room. The music workstation is in the middle of the image (vertically 35–55% from the top) with an EMPTY office chair turned towards the desk, its back to the viewer. Monitor screens are plain dark glowing rectangles with no text. Keep clear open floor space for a standing character: a free area on the rug in the centre-front and one beside the desk; any sofa or bed has a free empty seat. The top 15% of the image (ceiling) is calm and darker, and the bottom 12% is simple floor with no important objects (game UI will cover these areas). No people, no characters, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no UI buttons.
```

## NEGATIVE
```
people, person, character, human, face, crowd, text, letters, words, logo, watermark, signature, UI, button, frame, border, photorealistic, 3D render, blurry, low detail, distorted perspective, fisheye, cropped furniture at the centre, clutter on the floor in the centre, daylight, bright white walls
```
