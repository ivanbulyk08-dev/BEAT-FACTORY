## 1. Summary
Beat Factory is an offline idle game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email,
contacts, photos or location. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

## 2. Data stored on your device
Your game progress and settings (money, producers, artists, chosen language, sound settings) are saved **only on your
device**. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game uses Google AdMob to show ads (optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads).
To show and measure ads, Google may collect:
- the advertising identifier of your device (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow it),
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
- information about the ads you see and interact with.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Your choices
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** on first launch the game shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any
  time in **Settings → Privacy settings**.
- **Personalised ads:** you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings
  (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
- **Notifications:** reminders are optional and can be turned off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

## 5. Children
Beat Factory is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). [Adjust to your Google Play
"Target audience" choice. If you target children, AdMob must be configured for child-directed treatment.]

## 6. Data sharing and selling
We do not sell personal data. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google as described above, no data leaves your device.
